(01) · How it works

Four steps, and only the first is work.

  1. 01

    Teach it once

    Drop in your resume. Add your portfolio or LinkedIn if you like. Fill.ai builds a profile you can read and edit line by line.

  2. 02

    Open any form

    Press Alt Shift F or click the icon. A small panel reads every question, even inside embedded frames and Google Forms.

  3. 03

    It fills what it knows

    Names, email, phone, links, degree, graduation year, skills, dropdowns and the resume upload.

  4. 04

    You check, then submit

    Anything it isn't sure of waits for you. What you type yourself is never changed, and it's remembered for every form after.

(02) · Install

Two minutes, once.

Fill.ai isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so Chrome needs you to load it by hand. You only do this once.

  1. Download Fill.ai and unzip it

    On Windows, right click the file and choose Extract All. Put the folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents: Chrome runs Fill.ai from that folder.

  2. Open the extensions page

    Paste this into the address bar. Chrome doesn't let web pages link there.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Switch on Developer mode

    The switch is at the top right.

  4. Click Load unpacked

    Choose the unzipped fill-ai folder, the one with manifest.json inside.

  5. Set it up

    Fill.ai opens its settings by itself. Choose the AI, drop in your resume and press Build my profile.

(03) · The AI

Two of the three are free.

Pick one in settings and switch whenever you like. Your profile stays either way.

On this computer

Free · private

A model runs on your own graphics card through Ollama. Nothing about you leaves your computer.

  1. Install Ollama from ollama.com.
  2. Run this once in a terminal (3.4 GB): ollama pull qwen3.5:4b

Fill.ai finds it by itself. About 30 to 50 seconds a form on a laptop GPU.

Gemini

Free tier

Google's AI with a free key. Works on any laptop, no graphics card needed.

  1. Get a key at aistudio.google.com. No card needed.
  2. Paste it into Fill.ai's settings.

On the free tier, Google may use what you send, your resume included, to improve its products.

Claude

Paid

Claude Opus 5 with your own Anthropic key. The most careful answers, a few cents a form.

  1. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Paste it into Fill.ai's settings.

API use is billed separately from a Claude subscription.

(04) · Honest by design

It never makes things up.

An AI that fills forms is only useful if you can trust it. Fill.ai checks every answer in code before it touches the page, whichever AI gave it.

  • Every answer cites your profile

    No source, no fill. The field waits for you instead.

  • Facts are checked

    Emails and phone numbers must match yours exactly, and choices must be real options.

  • Personal questions stay yours

    Gender, caste, religion, disability and ID numbers are never filled on their own.

  • Agreements stay yours

    Terms, privacy policies and "I certify" boxes are never ticked for you.

  • Secrets are never read

    Passwords, OTPs, captchas and card details are skipped before anything is sent.

  • It never submits

    You check the form. You press submit.

(05) · Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Your profile, saved answers and keys live in your browser. When a form is filled, its questions and your profile go only to the AI you chose: nowhere at all on your computer, to Google with Gemini, to Anthropic with Claude.

No accounts, no analytics, and no server of mine in between.

Updating

When a new version is out.

  1. Download the zip again.
  2. Unzip it into the same folder and replace the files.
  3. On chrome://extensions, press the reload arrow on Fill.ai.

Your profile and saved answers stay. Unzipping to a different folder makes a new copy, so use Export profile in settings first and import it there.

Ready when you are.
